MOTS-c

A 16-amino-acid peptide encoded in mitochondrial DNA, studied as a mitochondria-derived signalling peptide.

Specifications

ClassSynthetic peptide, 16 residues
FormatLyophilized powder, sterile vial
Size listed10 mg
Typical lab useMitochondrial and cell-metabolism research

About this material

MOTS-c is notable because it is encoded by mitochondrial rather than nuclear DNA. Laboratories use the synthetic form in cell-based metabolic studies.

Handling

Store lyophilized vials sealed, dry, cold and away from light, and follow your institution’s written protocol. See storing lyophilized peptides.
