Retatrutide

A long-chain synthetic peptide that appears in the scientific literature as a multi-receptor agonist studied in metabolic research models.

ClassSynthetic peptide

About this material

Retatrutide is a synthetic peptide that researchers describe as acting on more than one incretin-family receptor. It is used in laboratory work to study receptor signalling in cell-based assays. This page covers the physical product only.

Handling

Store lyophilized vials sealed, dry, cold and away from light, and follow your institution’s written protocol. See storing lyophilized peptides.
