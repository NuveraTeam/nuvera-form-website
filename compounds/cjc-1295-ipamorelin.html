CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin

A combination of two synthetic peptides: a GHRH-analog sequence and a five-residue growth hormone secretagogue peptide.

Specifications

ClassTwo-peptide research blend
FormatLyophilized powder, sterile vial
Size listed10 mg
Typical lab useReceptor-signalling assays

About this material

This blend pairs two synthetic sequences commonly discussed in endocrine-receptor research. It is supplied as a lyophilized blend for in-vitro work.

Handling

Store lyophilized vials sealed, dry, cold and away from light, and follow your institution’s written protocol. See storing lyophilized peptides.
