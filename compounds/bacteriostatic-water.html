Bacteriostatic Water FAQ

What is bacteriostatic water?

Sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol, which inhibits bacterial growth in a multi-use vial.

How is it different from sterile water?

Plain sterile water has no preservative and is meant for single use; bacteriostatic water is designed for a multi-dose vial.
