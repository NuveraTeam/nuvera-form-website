Bacteriostatic Water

Sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a bacteriostatic preservative, used as a laboratory diluent.

About this material

Bacteriostatic water is sterile water with a small amount of benzyl alcohol that inhibits bacterial growth in a multi-use vial. Laboratories use it as a diluent according to their written protocols.

Handling

Store lyophilized vials sealed, dry, cold and away from light, and follow your institution’s written protocol. See storing lyophilized peptides.
