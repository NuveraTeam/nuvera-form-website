About Nuvera Form

A small Miami-based supplier of research peptides, with clear information and real people on support.

Who we are

Nuvera Form is a small, Miami-based supplier of research peptides and reconstitution reagents, run by a two-person team. We started it to offer a short list of compounds presented clearly, with honest information and real people answering the phone.

What we sell

A focused catalog of research peptides, research blends and bacteriostatic water, each with its own specification page. Every vial carries our own label showing the compound and quantity. Everything we sell is for laboratory research use only.

How we ship

Orders are packed in plain outer boxes and dispatched from the U.S. within 1–2 business days of payment. Ground shipping is a flat $25 and overnight is a flat $60. We email tracking when your order leaves, and replace damaged or incorrect items reported within 72 hours. Full details are on our shipping and returns page.

Documentation

We publish analytical documentation batch by batch and do not claim results we cannot show. Email us to ask what is available for your order.

How we sell

We sell only to buyers aged 21 or older who confirm research use, and we do not answer questions about dosing or use in people or animals. Read our research use policy.

Contact us

Email info@nuveraform.com or call 305-457-3814 during regular business hours. Follow us on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook at @nuveraform.
