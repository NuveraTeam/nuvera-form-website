Shipping & Returns

Dispatch times, delivery, costs and what to do if something is wrong.

Dispatch

Orders typically dispatch within 1–2 business days of payment.

Delivery

Domestic delivery is typically 3–5 business days after dispatch. A tracking link is emailed when your order leaves.

Payment

We currently accept payment by Zelle to (305) 457-3814. Place your order on the site, then send the total with your order number in the memo. Orders ship after payment is confirmed. Card payments may be added later.

Cost

Orders of $200 or more after discounts ship free. Smaller orders ship at a flat $15.

Damaged or incorrect items

Email info@nuveraform.com within 72 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will replace the item.

Returns

Because of the nature of the products, opened or used vials cannot be returned.
