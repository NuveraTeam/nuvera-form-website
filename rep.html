Posting guidelines

Keep it compliant
  • Lead with the code: FY305 for 20% off at nuveraform.com.
  • Disclose the partnership in every post (#ad or the paid partnership label).
  • Peptides are research use only. Show the brand, packaging, labels and unboxing.
  • Skincare can be shown in use on skin and hair. Keep claims cosmetic: smoother-looking, hydrated, fuller-looking.
  • No dosing, injecting, results, before/after or health and body claims for peptides. Never show research peptides on skin or in use.
  • No medical claims for skincare: avoid words like heals, treats, anti-inflammatory or regrows hair.
  • Do not combine codes or promise extra discounts.
