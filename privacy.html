Privacy Policy

How Nuvera Form collects, uses and protects the information you give us.

What we collect

Name, email address, phone number and field of research when you create an account; the email you give for newsletters or documentation requests; and order details once online checkout is available.

How we use it

To create your account, apply your welcome discount, reply to requests, send order updates and, if you opt in, product announcements.

Who processes it

Form submissions are handled by Web3Forms; email, including your subscriber record, is handled through Resend; the site is hosted on Vercel. These providers process data only to deliver their service.

Your choices

Email info@nuveraform.com to access, correct or delete your data, or to unsubscribe. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and storage

The site stores your age confirmation, cart and sign-in state in your browser’s local storage. It does not use advertising cookies.
