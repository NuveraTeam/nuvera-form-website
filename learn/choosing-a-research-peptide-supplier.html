How to choose a research peptide supplier

A checklist for comparing peptide vendors: documentation, labelling, shipping, payment and how they handle problems.

Clear labelling

Each vial should show the compound, quantity, lot number and a research-use-only statement. Vague names or missing lot numbers make traceability impossible.

Honest documentation

Ask what analytical documentation exists for the lot you will receive. A trustworthy supplier tells you what it has and what it does not, instead of posting generic certificates.

Responsible selling

Reputable research suppliers confirm buyer age and research intent and decline questions about dosing or human use. Health claims on a research supplier’s site are a red flag.

Shipping and replacements

Look for stated dispatch times, tracking, sensible packaging and a clear policy for damaged or incorrect items.
