Bacteriostatic water vs sterile water

What the two diluents contain, how they differ and how to choose between them for laboratory work.

What they are

Sterile water for injection is water with no preservative. Bacteriostatic water is sterile water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol added to inhibit bacterial growth.

Single use vs multi-use

Sterile water has no preservative, so a vial is generally treated as single use once opened. The benzyl alcohol in bacteriostatic water is what makes a multi-dose vial practical for repeated laboratory access.

Assay compatibility

Benzyl alcohol can affect some sensitive cell-based assays and certain analytical methods. If your assay is sensitive to preservatives, confirm the diluent in your protocol before use.
