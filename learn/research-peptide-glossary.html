Research peptide glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms you will see on research peptide labels and paperwork, from lyophilized to HPLC.

Lyophilized

Freeze-dried. Water is removed under low pressure and temperature, leaving a stable powder that is easier to ship and store.

RUO (research use only)

A label meaning the product is sold for laboratory investigation only, not as a drug, food, supplement or cosmetic, and not for use in people or animals. See what research use only means.

Bacteriostatic water

Sterile water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol, used as a multi-use laboratory diluent. See bacteriostatic water.

HPLC

High-performance liquid chromatography, the method most often used to report a peptide’s purity. See how to read a certificate of analysis.

Mass spectrometry (MS)

A technique that measures molecular mass, used to confirm that the main component is the expected compound.

Blend

A single vial containing more than one peptide, such as the BPC-157 / TB-500 blend or CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin.

Lot or batch number

The identifier that ties a vial to the production run and any analytical documentation for it.

Copper peptide

A peptide bound to a copper ion, such as GHK-Cu.
