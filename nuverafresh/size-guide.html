Finding the right size makes all the difference in shapewear. Take two minutes to measure — it's the best way to get a comfortable, smooth fit.

How to measure

Use a soft measuring tape. Measure over bare skin or thin underwear, standing straight and relaxed. Keep the tape snug but not tight, and parallel to the floor. Breathe normally — don't hold your breath or pull in your stomach.

  1. Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust, wearing a non-padded bra.
  2. Waist: Measure around your natural waist — the narrowest part of your torso, usually 1–2 inches above your belly button.
  3. Hips: Stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  4. Torso (optional, helpful for bodysuits): Measure from the top of one shoulder, down the front, through your legs, and up the back to the same shoulder. This helps if you are very tall or petite.

Choosing your size

  • Use your waist measurement first. If your hips fall in a different size, choose based on your hips for bodysuits.
  • Between sizes? Choose the larger size for all-day comfort.
  • Don't size down for extra control. Shapewear that is too small can roll, dig in, or feel restrictive.
  • Long torso? If your torso measurement is on the long side, consider sizing up.
  • Your first size exchange is free — see our Returns & Exchanges Policy.

Fit notes

  • The bodysuit should feel snug and supportive, not painful. It may feel firm the first few wears.
  • The waist band should lie flat without rolling when you sit and stand.
  • The gusset should sit comfortably without pulling. The snap/open-gusset closure should fasten easily.
  • If you feel numbness, tingling, or shortness of breath, remove it and see our Product Disclaimer.
