Please read this before wearing any NUVERA FRESH product.

Shapewear is not a medical device

NUVERA FRESH bodysuits are everyday fashion shapewear. They are designed to smooth and shape the look of your silhouette under clothing. They are not medical devices and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. They are not medical-grade compression garments.

Not for post-surgical or medical compression

Do not use our products as a post-surgical garment (for example, after liposuction, a tummy tuck, a BBL, or a C-section), as a medical compression garment, or as a back, posture, or hernia support unless your doctor has specifically told you that this product is appropriate for you. If you need compression after surgery or for a medical condition, follow your doctor's instructions and use the garment they recommend.

No weight-loss or body-changing claims

Our shapewear changes how your clothes look while you are wearing it. It does not cause weight loss, burn fat, reduce inches permanently, "train" your waist, or change your body shape over time. We don't use before-and-after images. Photos and videos we share show how the garment looks while worn and are not a promise of results.

Results and appearance vary

The smoothing effect, comfort, and fit depend on your body shape, measurements, the size you choose, and how you wear it. Photos and videos of models or creators show individual experiences and may differ from yours. Colors can look different on different screens.

Fit and sizing

  • Choose your size using our Size Guide. Shapewear should feel snug and supportive, never painful.
  • Do not size down to get "more control." A size that is too small can roll, dig in, or restrict breathing and circulation.
  • If you are between sizes, we generally recommend the larger size for all-day wear.

Wear safely

Stop wearing the garment and remove it right away if you feel:

  • Pain, pinching, or pressure that doesn't go away
  • Numbness, tingling, or "pins and needles"
  • Shortness of breath, dizziness, or lightheadedness
  • Swelling, skin color changes, or marks that do not fade
  • Heartburn, nausea, or stomach discomfort
  • Skin irritation, rash, or itching

If symptoms continue after you remove the garment, contact a healthcare professional.

General tips:

  • Start with a few hours at a time and build up gradually.
  • Take breaks, and do not sleep in the garment unless a doctor advises it.
  • Avoid wearing it during intense exercise unless you find it comfortable.
  • Stay hydrated, and use the snap/open-gusset closure for bathroom breaks rather than "holding it."

Talk to a professional first if you:

  • Are pregnant, trying to become pregnant, or recently gave birth
  • Have recently had surgery
  • Have circulation problems, blood clots (or a history of them), varicose veins, diabetes, nerve conditions, lymphedema, heart or lung conditions, acid reflux/GERD, digestive or bladder conditions, or sensitive skin or skin conditions
  • Have any other medical condition, or if you are unsure whether shapewear is right for you

Allergies and materials

Check the fabric content on the product page and garment label before wearing. Do not wear the product if you are sensitive or allergic to any listed material (for example, spandex/elastane or latex, if listed).

Care

Follow the care label. Improper washing or drying (for example, high heat) can damage the fabric and closures.
