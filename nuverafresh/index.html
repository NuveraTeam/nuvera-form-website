Size & fit

Size & fit guide Pending sample testing

How to measure

Use a soft measuring tape. Measure over light underwear, keep the tape level and snug but not tight, and breathe normally.

  1. Bust: measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape straight across your back.
  2. Waist: measure around your natural waist, the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.
  3. Hips: stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  4. Between sizes? Sizing advice for in-between measurements will be added after sample testing.
