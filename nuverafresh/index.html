Questions, answered.

Can’t find yours? See all FAQs or contact us.

How does the snap closure work?

The gusset fastens with snaps. Unsnap it to use the restroom, then snap it closed again, without taking off your top, dress or the bodysuit itself.

When can I order?

Orders open on launch day, December 7, 2026. Until then nothing can be bought or reserved. Join the launch list and we’ll email you when it goes live.

What sizes and colors will it come in?

Sizes XS to 3XL, in Black, Nude and Cocoa. See the size guide for how to measure yourself.

How much will it cost?

$44.99 for one bodysuit, or $74.99 for a 2-pack in any two colors ($14.99 less than two singles at $89.98). US orders of $50 or more ship free, so the 2-pack ships free. A single bodysuit ships for a $4.99 flat rate.

Is this a medical or compression garment?

No. It’s everyday shapewear, designed to smooth and sculpt the look of your silhouette under fitted clothes. It isn’t a medical, post-surgical or weight-loss product.

Can I return or exchange it?

Yes, within the window in our returns & exchanges policy. For hygiene, items must be unworn with tags and the hygienic liner still attached.
