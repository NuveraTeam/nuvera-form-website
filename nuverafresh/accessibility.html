Prelaunch note. This prelaunch site is hand-built to WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus states, text alternatives for illustrations, AA color contrast and reduced motion when your device asks for it. The statement below also covers our store once it opens.

NUVERA FRESH ({{BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME}}) wants everyone to be able to shop on nuverafresh.com, including people with disabilities.

Our goal

We aim for our website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, a widely used standard for web accessibility.

What we do

  • Use a modern Shopify theme built with accessibility features such as keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and screen-reader-friendly structure
  • Add descriptive alternative (alt) text to product images
  • Keep text readable with sufficient color contrast and resizable text
  • Use clear headings, labels, and button names
  • Provide captions or text descriptions for key product videos when possible
  • Offer our size guide and policies as text (not only as images)
  • Provide key customer pages in English and Spanish

Known limitations

Some third-party features (such as payment pop-ups, review widgets, chat tools, or embedded social media) are provided by other companies and may not fully meet our goal. We choose partners with accessibility in mind and work to fix issues we find.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of our website, or need information in a different format, please email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with:

  • The page web address (URL)
  • A description of the problem
  • The device, browser, and assistive technology you use (if any)

We will respond within 2 business days and work with you to provide the information or complete your order through another method, such as by email.

Ongoing effort

We review our website regularly, including after adding new features or apps, and update this statement as we improve.
