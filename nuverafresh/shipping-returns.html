Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States, including APO/FPO/DPO military addresses and P.O. boxes. We do not ship internationally at this time.

Processing time

  • Orders are packed and shipped within 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays) after you place them.
  • Orders placed after 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time, or on a weekend or holiday, begin processing the next business day.
  • During launches, sales, and the holiday season, processing can take up to 3 business days. If it will take longer, we will email you (see "If your order is delayed" below).

Shipping method and rates

We ship with USPS Ground Advantage, which USPS typically delivers in 2–5 business days after the package is shipped. Delivery estimates are provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed.

Order subtotal (after discounts, before tax) Shipping cost
Under $50.00 $4.99 flat rate
$50.00 or more Free

Faster shipping (such as USPS Priority Mail) may be offered at checkout when available; the price will be shown before you pay.

Order confirmation and tracking

You will receive an order confirmation email right after checkout and a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number when your label is created. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after the carrier scans your package.

Holiday shipping (December 2026)

USPS recommends these send-by dates for delivery by December 25 within the contiguous United States:

  • USPS Ground Advantage: December 17, 2026
  • USPS Priority Mail: December 18, 2026

To give your order the best chance of arriving by December 25, place it by December 15, 2026 for standard shipping, or by December 16, 2026 if Priority Mail is offered at checkout. These are carrier recommendations, not guarantees; USPS delays during peak season are outside our control. Alaska, Hawaii, and military addresses have earlier USPS dates.

If your order is delayed

If we cannot ship your order within the time stated above, we will email you with a new expected ship date and give you the option to cancel for a full refund. If you cancel, we will refund your full payment, including shipping, to your original payment method within 7 business days.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us right away at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We can update the address only if the order has not shipped yet. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you to reship it (a new shipping charge may apply) or refund the item price.

Lost, stolen, or damaged packages

Package shows "delivered" but you can't find it

  1. Check around your home, mailbox, parcel lockers, and with neighbors or your building office.
  2. Wait 48 hours; packages are sometimes scanned as delivered before they arrive.
  3. If it still hasn't appeared, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 7 days of the delivery date with your order number. We will open a claim with USPS and help you file a USPS Missing Mail search.

Package hasn't moved in tracking

If tracking hasn't updated for 7 business days, or your package hasn't arrived within 10 business days of shipping, email us with your order number. We will contact USPS and, if the package is confirmed lost, we will send a replacement (if in stock) or issue a full refund.

Package arrived damaged

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the outer package, the packaging inside, and the item. Please keep the packaging until your claim is resolved. If the item is damaged, we will send a replacement or issue a refund, at no cost to you.

Taxes

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address, as required by law.

Questions

Email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 1 business day.
