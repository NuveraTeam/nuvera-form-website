The policy below describes how we will handle information once our store opens on December 7, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how {{BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME}} ("NUVERA FRESH," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit nuverafresh.com (the "Site"), buy from us, or contact us. Our store is hosted on Shopify. By using the Site, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order information: name, billing and shipping address, email address, phone number (if you provide it), items purchased, order notes, and size/fit details you share with us.
  • Account information (if you create an account): name, email, password (stored securely by Shopify), order history, and saved addresses.
  • Customer support: messages, photos (for example, of a damaged item), and other information you send us by email, chat, or social media.
  • Marketing sign-ups: your email address (and phone number if you opt in to texts) when you join our list.
  • Reviews: content you post in a product review, including your name or initials as displayed.

Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment providers, including Shopify Payments (Stripe), Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. These providers receive your card or account details directly. We do not see or store your full card number. We receive limited details such as the card type, last four digits, and billing address to process and protect orders.

Information collected automatically

When you browse the Site, Shopify and our tools automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location, pages viewed, referring website, and how you interact with the Site. We collect this using cookies, pixels, and similar technologies (see Section 4).

Information from others

  • TikTok, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and other platforms may share ad-performance information with us, such as whether you clicked an ad.
  • If you buy through TikTok Shop, TikTok shares the order information we need to fulfill your order. TikTok's own privacy policy also applies to purchases made in the TikTok app.

2. How we use your information

  • Process, ship, and track your orders; handle returns, exchanges, and refunds
  • Communicate with you about your order and respond to questions
  • Prevent fraud and protect the security of our store and customers
  • Send marketing emails or texts only if you opted in (see Section 5)
  • Improve the Site, products, and customer experience, including through analytics
  • Show you relevant ads on other platforms (see Section 4)
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax and accounting requirements

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as needed with:

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and processes data on our behalf (see Shopify's privacy policy at shopify.com/legal/privacy)
  • Payment processors listed above, to complete payments and prevent fraud
  • Shipping carriers and label services (such as USPS, Shopify Shipping, Pirate Ship, or TikTok Shipping) to deliver your order
  • Email and marketing tools (such as Shopify Messaging / Shopify Email) to send messages you agreed to receive
  • Advertising and analytics partners (such as TikTok, Meta, and Google), as described in Section 4
  • Professional advisers (accountants, lawyers) and authorities when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect rights and safety
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or transferred

4. Cookies, pixels, and analytics

We and our partners use cookies and pixels to make the Site work (for example, to keep items in your cart), to understand how visitors use the Site (Shopify analytics), and to measure and personalize ads (for example, the TikTok Pixel and Meta Pixel). Sharing data with advertising partners for targeted advertising may be considered "sharing" or "selling" under some state privacy laws.

Your choices:

  • Use the cookie/privacy banner or the "Your privacy choices" link in our footer to opt out of non-essential and advertising cookies.
  • We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as an opt-out of targeted advertising where Shopify supports it.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some Site features may not work without them.
  • You can also adjust ad preferences in your TikTok, Meta, and Google account settings.

5. Email and text marketing

  • We send marketing emails only to people who sign up or check the marketing box at checkout.
  • Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to unsubscribe. We process unsubscribe requests promptly and within 10 business days.
  • If you opt in to text messages, message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of purchase.
  • Even if you unsubscribe from marketing, we will still send transactional messages about your orders.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know/access the personal information we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information (subject to legal exceptions, such as tax records)
  • Get a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, "sale," or "sharing" of personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

California residents: The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended, gives California residents the rights above where it applies. We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

Florida residents: We do not sell sensitive personal data. Florida residents may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, and we will respond in good faith.

Residents of other states with consumer privacy laws may exercise the rights those laws provide.

How to make a request: Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Privacy Request" and tell us what you are asking for. We will verify your request by matching information you provide (such as your email and an order number) with our records, and respond within 45 days. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of authorization. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy Appeal."

7. Data retention

We keep order and transaction records for as long as needed to fulfill orders and to meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements (generally up to 7 years). We keep marketing data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and support messages for up to 3 years.

8. Security

We use Shopify's secure, PCI-compliant checkout and limit access to personal information to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If changes are significant, we will provide additional notice, such as on the Site or by email.
