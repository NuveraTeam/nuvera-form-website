We want your NUVERA FRESH bodysuit to fit and feel right. Because shapewear is an intimate apparel item, we follow hygiene rules that protect every customer. Please read this policy before you buy.

This policy applies to orders placed on nuverafresh.com. Orders placed on TikTok Shop must be returned through TikTok Shop's return process in the TikTok app, under TikTok Shop's return policy.

Free first size exchange (30 days)

Not the right size? Your first size exchange per order is free. We will email you a prepaid return label and ship the new size at no charge once your return is scanned by the carrier (or once we receive it, if the new size is out of stock).

  • Exchanges are for a different size of the same item. If you'd like a different color, tell us when you request the exchange and we will do our best, subject to stock.
  • If the size you need is out of stock, we will offer a different color in your size or a full refund for the returned item.
  • A second exchange on the same item is treated as a return and new order.

Returns for refund (30 days)

You may return eligible items within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method.

  • Return shipping: For refunds, a $4.99 return label fee is deducted from your refund if you use the prepaid label we provide. You may also ship the return yourself with tracking at your own cost.
  • Original shipping charges are not refundable, except when the item is defective, damaged, or we sent the wrong item.
  • If you received free shipping on an order of $50 or more and your return brings the kept total below $50, we do not charge you for the original shipping.
  • Refund timing: We inspect returns within 3 business days of arrival and issue approved refunds within 2 business days after inspection. Your bank or card issuer may take 5–10 business days to post the refund.

Condition requirements (hygiene rules)

To be eligible for an exchange or refund, items must be:

  • Unworn, except for a brief try-on over your own underwear
  • Unwashed, with no stains, odors, deodorant, makeup, self-tanner, lotion, perfume, or pet hair
  • Returned with all original tags attached
  • Returned with the hygienic liner/sticker in the gusset area intact and unremoved
  • Returned in the original packaging, or similar protective packaging

Items that do not meet these conditions cannot be resold and will be sent back to you (at your shipping cost) or, if you prefer, recycled. No refund is issued for them.

Final sale items

The following are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged, except if defective, damaged, or incorrect:

  • Items clearly marked "Final Sale" on the product page at the time of purchase
  • Items purchased at 40% off or more (we will state this on the sale page)
  • Items worn, washed, altered, or with the hygienic liner or tags removed

Bundles (such as the 2-pack) can be exchanged for size. A bundle may be returned for a refund only as a full bundle, with both items meeting the conditions above.

Defective, damaged, or wrong items

If your item arrives with a defect (for example, a broken snap or hook, a seam opening, or a fabric flaw), is damaged in shipping, or is not what you ordered:

  1. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 30 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the issue.
  2. Please do not wash or alter the item.
  3. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including original shipping, and provide a prepaid return label if we need the item back.

Defects that appear after normal wear within 30 days of delivery are also covered. Normal wear and tear, damage from improper washing or drying, and damage from misuse are not considered defects.

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Return" or "Exchange," your order number, the item, the reason, and (for exchanges) the size you need.
  2. We will reply within 1 business day with instructions and, where applicable, a prepaid label. Returns sent without contacting us first may be delayed.
  3. Pack the item with tags and liner intact, attach the label, and drop it off at USPS within 7 days of receiving your label.
  4. We will email you when your return is received and when your refund or exchange is processed.

Please send returns only to the address we provide in your return instructions: {{BUSINESS_ADDRESS}}.

Gifts

Gift recipients can exchange for size within 30 days of delivery. Refunds for gifts go to the original purchaser's payment method.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.
