The bodysuit

What is the Sculpt Bodysuit?

An everyday sculpting bodysuit, designed to smooth the look of your silhouette under fitted clothes, with an easy-off snap gusset so you don't have to take it off to use the bathroom.

How does the bathroom-friendly closure work?

The gusset (the panel between the legs) opens so you can use the restroom without taking off the bodysuit or your outfit. [Owner: update this answer after samples arrive to match the actual closure — snaps, hooks, or an open gusset — and how many.]

Will it show under my clothes?

It's designed to be worn under everyday outfits. We recommend the Nude or Cocoa shade closest to your skin tone under light or thin fabrics, and Black under dark clothing. As with any shapewear, very sheer or very tight fabrics may show outlines.

Is it comfortable to wear all day?

Comfort depends on fit and personal preference. Start with a few hours, choose the correct size, and take breaks. See our Product Disclaimer for safe-wear tips.

Will it help me lose weight?

No. Shapewear changes how your clothes look while you wear it. It does not cause weight loss or permanently change your shape.

Can I wear it after surgery or while pregnant?

Our bodysuit is not a medical or post-surgical garment. If you've had surgery, are pregnant, or have a medical condition, please ask your doctor before wearing any shapewear.

What is it made of?

See the fabric content on the product page and the garment label. [Owner: add fiber content and country of origin from the garment label before launch.]

Sizing

How do I find my size?

Measure your bust, waist, and hips and compare them with our Size Guide. Use your waist first; if your hips are in a larger size, choose based on your hips.

I'm between sizes. What should I choose?

Choose the larger size for all-day comfort. Don't size down for extra control — it can cause rolling and discomfort.

What sizes and colors do you offer?

Sizes XS–3XL in Black, Nude, and Cocoa (availability may vary by size and color).

What if it doesn't fit?

Your first size exchange is free within 30 days of delivery, as long as the item is unworn with tags and the hygienic liner intact. See our Returns & Exchanges Policy.

Washing and care

How do I wash it?

Follow the care label. In general, shapewear lasts longest when you hand wash or machine wash cold on a gentle cycle in a mesh laundry bag, with mild detergent. Do not use bleach or fabric softener. Lay flat or hang to dry — do not tumble dry or iron. [Owner: confirm against the actual care label.]

How often should I wash it?

Wash after every one or two wears to keep it fresh and help the fabric keep its shape.

Studio: grip socks and the Studio Set

What are the Studio Grip Socks?

Crew-length, full-toe socks with a silicone-dot grip sole, made for reformer and mat Pilates, barre and yoga. They come in Black, Nude and Cocoa to match the bodysuit. $14 a pair, or 3 pairs for $34 in any colors.

When can I order them?

They're planned to launch with the Sculpt Bodysuit on December 7, 2026, as long as our samples pass testing in time. If they don't, they'll follow soon after, and we'll update this page.

What size are the grip socks?

One size, planned to fit women's US shoe sizes 5 to 10. [Owner: confirm the size range with the supplier and on the samples before launch.]

Do grip socks stop me from slipping?

The silicone dots are designed to add grip on studio floors and reformer platforms, but grip depends on the surface, and grip socks aren't safety equipment. Follow your studio's and instructor's guidance.

How do I wash the grip socks?

Follow the care label. In general, grip socks last longest turned inside out, machine washed cold on a gentle cycle, and air dried. Skip bleach and fabric softener, which can coat the grip dots. [Owner: confirm against the actual care label.]

Can I return grip socks?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery if they're unworn and unwashed, with tags and original packaging. For hygiene, worn socks can't be returned unless they're defective. See our Returns & Exchanges Policy.

What is the Studio Set, and when is it coming?

Seamless Pilates leggings and a matching sports bra, planned for January 2027 in Black and Cocoa, sizes XS–3XL. Planned prices are $54 for the set, $34 for the leggings and $26 for the bra. It isn't available to order yet, and details may change after we test samples.

Returns and exchanges

Can I return it?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery, if it's unworn (tried on over underwear only), unwashed, and has tags and the hygienic liner intact. Return shipping is deducted from refunds; your first size exchange is free.

Why the hygiene rules?

Shapewear is intimate apparel. These rules make sure every customer receives a brand-new, unworn item.
